# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (in progress)
- **Settings file reorganised** (2026-11-28): one section per area (`[Display]`, `[Interface]`, `[Text]`, `[Fonts]`, `[Debug]`). Text sizes are one `[Text] Size` plus optional sizes by category (`[Text.Menus]`, `[Text.Screens]`, `[Text.Game]`, each with an `All` and items per screen or area; 0 = follow the level above). Fonts are one switch, `[Fonts] All=Sharp | Original`, with an optional choice per font (`Names`, `Tooltips`, `Journal`, `Menu`, `Interface`, `Numbers`, `Credits`); a font of your own for names or menus is just its `.ttf` / `.otf` file (face and weight are read from the file). The text hook is now always on (`[Debug] TextHook=0` only for troubleshooting); the font-texture switches (`FontPages`, `FontRedraw`, `FontFace`/`FontFile`/`FontWeight`, `FontOwnMetrics`, `…FontFace/File/Weight`) are gone and turn on by themselves when needed; `HudMouse` moved to `[Debug]`. Settings that cannot be used are named in the log. The keys of earlier development builds (`[UI]`, `…TextScale`, `…Font`) are not read any more: the entries below still use those names; `docs/DEVLOG.md` (2026-11-28) maps each to its new place.
- Menus, inventory, character, journal and other full-screen screens at their original 4:3 shape, centered with black bars.
- In-game HUD re-placed with correct proportions: `Hud` = `split` (default), `right`, `center` or `stretched`.
- `MaxAspect`: optional aspect-ratio cap; wider screens are pillarboxed to it.
- Larger mouse cursor (`CursorSize`); the gameplay cursor and its tooltips always follow the real mouse.
- **HUD clicks and tooltips match the moved HUD** (on by default; `[Debug] HudMouse=0` turns it off): in gameplay the game is given the HUD button under the mouse at its original position, and the cursor and its tooltip are drawn at the real mouse. Works with every `HudLayout` and `MaxAspect`.
- **Bigger tooltips** (`TextScale`, default 200%): tooltips and labels next to the cursor (HUD, menus, inventory items, world objects) and the one-line messages above the HUD ("Empty.", "Gained object: …") are scaled with their text, margins and distance to the cursor, and kept on screen. Multi-line panels and other text are not scaled yet.
- Menu tooltips move together with their text (no more text drifting out of its box); tooltip boxes keep their shape.
- Debug log (`Log=1`):
  - one log per session (`SoulbringerModern_<exe>_<pid>.log`); automatic capture of every new screen (`AutoCapture`), mode events;
  - hover events: every tooltip and object label with cursor position, box size and offset, text size, margins, the words and the world objects under the cursor; a text catalogue (UTF-16 game text supported);
  - click events with the cursor, screen region, label and object under the cursor. In gameplay the game reads the mouse through DirectInput; the log now reads the buttons there too.
  - `ScreenshotKey` (default F11): saves a BMP of the game to `Screenshots\` in the game folder, plus a frame capture of the same frame in the log.
  - the game's text is decoded back into words from the drawn glyphs and the game's fonts: hover labels, a `text:` catalogue of every line shown, and the characters of each glyph batch in frame captures (replaces a stack walk that mostly found unrelated strings).
  - per gameplay click, an `aim:` line: the HUD part drawn under the cursor, where the game has that point, where it actually hit-tests, and the miss in px; and `event: click result` 20 frames later (menu opened, screen changed, or no change).
- **Fix (dev build only):** the first DirectInput click hook crashed the game after the intro (it hooked the keyboard and replaced the device's vtable). It now patches only two vtable entries, filters to the mouse, and restores them on unload.
- Regression tests: recorded sessions replayed in order through the shipped layout code under 8 HUD/aspect configurations; `tools/sbm_log.py` reads logs (hover timeline, size summary, text catalogue, before/after pictures).
- HUD click mapping tests: round trip (game point -> drawn -> game point) for every layout and cap at 16:9, 21:9 and 16:10; the in-game measured offsets; every recorded gameplay click replayed and, for new logs, compared with the DLL's `aim:` lines.

- Where the world now shows through the old stretched HUD area (beside the bar, left of the column), the game no longer finds its HUD there (no second "Compass" tooltip); world clicks there act at the nearest point above the bar line or left of the column line.
- Tooltip fixes found in play: inventory item tooltips are enlarged and stay over their item; HUD slot labels under a tooltip keep their size; the name of a character under the cursor stays inside its box when the box is moved off the larger cursor (it was left behind, and part of it jumped to the HUD column); a tooltip near the menu's edge stays inside the menu; no small tooltip flashing at the HUD's old position while the mouse moves (tooltips are matched to the game's cursor of the same frame).
- Text decoding tests (with the game's fonts available): every glyph of all 16 fonts, clipped glyphs, lines joined across batches.

- **Experimental, off by default (`[Debug] TextHook=1`):** the game itself draws all its text at `TextScale`: fonts are enlarged when the game loads them and each letter still shows its own part of the font texture, so wrapping, box sizes and line spacing follow the larger text. Replaces the tooltip stretching while it is on. Three code patches, each checked against the original bytes first and removed when the renderer unloads.

- **Experimental (`[Debug] FontOwnMetrics=1`, default, with TextHook, FontPages and `FontRedraw=phil,hbut`):** the redrawn journal and tooltip fonts get their own glyph cells and widths (packed into free space on the font textures; letter spacing and kerning of these two fonts set to 0), so Cardo is drawn at full size with its natural spacing instead of squeezed into the original letters' rectangles.
- **Labels under a tooltip stay put:** with the text hook, only the tooltip's own text moves with its box (e.g. the COMBO slot label no longer jumps along with the "Combo screen" tooltip).
- **Tooltip boxes never cover the cursor:** with larger text (or a larger cursor) a tooltip box that would overlap the cursor is moved above it (below it at the top of the screen; inventory item tooltips below the cursor).
- **Text size per kind of text** (`[UI] JournalTextScale`, `TooltipTextScale`, `MenuTextScale`, `InterfaceTextScale`, `NameTextScale`, `NumberTextScale`, `CreditsTextScale`; 0 = `TextScale`): with `[Debug] TextHook=1` each kind (by the game font it uses) is laid out at its own size, so e.g. tooltips can be 300% while menus whose panels cannot hold larger text stay at 200%. The ini lists exactly what each kind covers (from the text the game was seen drawing); a kind changes every place its font is used. Without the text hook, `TooltipTextScale` sizes the stretched cursor tooltips.
- **Experimental, off by default (`[Debug] FontPages=1`):** the four font textures are created larger (`FontPageScale`, default `TextScale`/100 rounded up) and with full alpha (A8R8G8B8 instead of 1-bit alpha), and the game's font pages are enlarged into them. No visible change intended; it prepares sharper redrawn fonts. No code patches: only two renderer calls get different arguments, after a check of the renderer build.

- **Experimental, off by default (`[Debug] FontRedraw=phil,hbut`, needs `FontPages=1`):** the journal font (`phil`) and the tooltip font (`hbut`) are redrawn from a TrueType face into the larger font textures, with smooth edges: default Cardo Bold (`FontFace`, `FontWeight`, `FontFile`), shipped in `SoulbringerModern\fonts\` under the SIL Open Font License and loaded for the game only (nothing installed in Windows). Fallbacks: Constantia, Palatino Linotype, Book Antiqua.

- **Experimental, off by default (`[Debug] FontPlaces=1`, needs `TextHook=1`):** every place that draws with a shared font (interface 16xy: options menu, character screen, inventory numbers, HUD column labels…; menu magc: main menu, save list, conversations…; tooltip hbut; names mgrn; messages mred; the HUD column's selected and highlighted move labels, 16xb/16xx) gets its own copy of the font, still at its current size, as the first step towards a separate text size per place. Changes the font address read by 98 instructions of the game (each checked first, restored on unload). With `Log=1`, `place:` lines name the font copy or font object behind every new piece of text, and the text object, its entry in the game's shared text-object pool and its position.

- **Text size per screen or area** (`[UI] MainMenuTextScale`, `SaveListTextScale`, `OptionsTextScale`, `CharacterTextScale`, `MapTextScale`, `HudLabelTextScale`, `LabelTextScale`, `PlaceNameTextScale`, `NpcNameTextScale`, `MessageTextScale`; experimental, needs `TextHook=1`): finer control than the per-kind sizes. Each changes only its own screen's text, laid out by the game at that size (wrapping and boxes follow); 0 = the kind's size (e.g. `OptionsTextScale=0` follows `InterfaceTextScale`). `HudLabelTextScale` includes the selected move label. Conversations and the quit dialog follow `MenuTextScale` until a session identifies them.

- **Sharp interface font** (`[UI] InterfaceFont=1`, experimental): the small pixel font of the options menu, stats page, inventory numbers, HUD move labels and combo screen is redrawn from Montserrat ExtraBold (bundled, SIL Open Font License) in the game's own colours (yellow face with a red band, magenta and dark outline; the selected label purple, the cut-out label kept cut out), at high resolution, in the same places and spacing. `InterfaceFont=0` shows the game's original font for comparison.
- **Sharp combat numbers** (`[UI] NumberFont=Montserrat`, default): the damage numbers over characters in a fight (red "-16", green "0" for a miss) redrawn from Montserrat ExtraBold in the game's yellow/red and green colours with a one-texel outline, in the game's own places and spacing; `Original` = the game's pixel digits. Size: `NumberTextScale`.
- **Sharp credits font** (`[UI] CreditsFont=Credits`, default): the large white letters of the credits and "GAME OVER" (cred) redrawn from **Soulbringer Credits**, the user's upscale of the game's letters, bundled under the SIL Open Font License, in the game's own places and spacing; `Original` = the game's letters. Size: `CreditsTextScale`.

- **Soulbringer Text: the game's own text font, sharp** (`[UI] NameFont`, `TooltipFont`, `JournalFont` = `Text`): the game draws names under the cursor, pickup messages, "Locked." / "Empty.", tooltips, place names, HUD bar labels and the journal in one typeface (the tooltip, green and red fonts have identical letters; the journal's are one pixel larger). All of them are now redrawn from **Soulbringer Text**, an upscaled outline version of that font made for this project (bundled, SIL Open Font License), each in its own colour and at its own size. `Original` shows the game's letters; `Cardo` keeps the earlier Cardo Bold for tooltips/journal; `NameFont=Custom` takes a face of your own. The names need `TextHook=1`. Letters missing from the face keep the game's.
- **Conversation screen** (with `Layout=1`): the NPC portrait keeps its square shape (it was stretched to the wide screen) and sits at the top right, with the dialogue box and its text next to it as before; the brief flicker of the portrait when the dialogue moves to its next line is covered by drawing the last portrait in the one frame where the game skips it (provisional, may be the original behaviour: `[Debug] ConversationPortraitHold=0` turns it off).
- **Street talk** (guards and passers-by, no letterbox): the portrait is square too, centred where it sits in the 4:3 picture, and the speech box beside it moves with it (needs `Layout=1`).
- **Quest message centred:** "A new quest has been added to your journal." is centred on the screen over the HUD bar like the other messages (the game centres it on the view left of the HUD column; needs `Layout=1`).
- **Conversation text size** (`[UI] ConversationTextScale`, needs `TextHook=1`): what the character says and your reply choices, sized apart from the main menu and save list (0 = `MenuTextScale`).
- **Menu font spacing:** the menu and conversation letters (Soulbringer Uncial) keep their real width; they were squeezed by one texel each to leave room for an outline that is invisible on the dark backgrounds, which made the text look letter-spaced ("r e f r e s h m e n t").
- **Main menu and save list with enlarged text** (with `TextHook=1`): the copyright line stays at the bottom right of the main menu, where the game puts it at 1024×768 (it moved to the left edge as the text grew); the save list shows the whole date and time instead of cutting it with "...".
- **Sharp menu font** (`[UI] MenuFont=EagleLake`, experimental, needs `TextHook=1`): the calligraphic font of the main menu, save/load list and quit dialog is redrawn from Eagle Lake (bundled as "Soulbringer Uncial": Eagle Lake with its hanging old-style digits changed to lining figures that sit on the line; SIL Open Font License), the closest match to the original letters, in the game's yellow; `MenuFont=MedievalSharp` (also bundled) is the plainer alternative, `MenuFont=Custom` takes a face of your own from `[Debug] MenuFontFace`/`MenuFontFile` (for fonts you may use but not share), `MenuFont=0` the original letters. The letters get their own taller cells and natural widths, like the journal and tooltip fonts.

- **Enlarged labels stay on one line** (with `TextHook=1`): the game's automatic box sizing broke short labels onto two lines once their font was enlarged ("TOOL / TIPS", "SOUND / FX" in the options menu, "HIT / POINTS" on the stats page, "CUT / LOW" in the HUD column), although the panels have room. Such labels now get a one-line box measured by the game's own layout. Affects only enlarged fonts; tooltips and multi-line text are unchanged.

- **Fixed:** inventory items and the weapon icon in the HUD column were drawn a third too big on widescreen screens (the game sizes these 3D models by the screen width), so an item covered its own stack count. They now have their original size next to their slots.

- `[Debug] Stock=1`: turns every enhancement off at once (widescreen, layout, cursor, HUD mouse, tooltip and text scaling) while keeping the log and the screenshot key, for reference screenshots of the unmodified game.

- Debug log (`Log=1`): the four font textures (renderer slots 0x4E–0x51) are logged and their first upload saved as `SBM_fontpage_<slot>_<w>x<h>x<bpp>.raw` in the game folder (research for sharper fonts; read only).
- Debug log (`Log=1`), font surface probe (read only): the converted font textures' size and pixel format, a checksum of every upload, whether they are lost and restored (alt-tab), the formats of the other textures, and `ViewTexture`/`OsTaskSwitch`/`SetGlobalPalette` calls; the first converted page saved as `SBM_fontsurf_<slot>.raw` and the palette as `SBM_palette.raw`. `tools/font_surface.py` renders and checks them. Also the renderer's list of texture formats it can create (`format list:` lines).

Known issues: some text has no size setting of its own yet and follows its category's `All` (quit dialog, interface text not identified yet); fixed panels such as the options menu can cut off text that is too large. Only 4K 16:9 has been play-tested.

## 0.1.1
- **Fix:** the game loads the renderer twice (a hardware check, then for real). 0.1.0 left its widescreen patch pointing into the first, unloaded copy of the DLL. It only worked if Windows reloaded the DLL at the same address, and could crash otherwise. The patch is now removed when the DLL unloads, and a leftover patch is recognised and re-pointed.
- Debug log (`Log=1`): F9 now also records one complete frame (every draw call with texture, position and depth), for UI work.
